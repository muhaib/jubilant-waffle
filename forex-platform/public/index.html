How it works

Nine steps from signing up to withdrawing

Nothing is credited automatically. A deposit becomes a balance only after a person on our team has matched it against our receiving account records.

1

Create your account

Your name, email and mobile number. You are issued an internal account ID such as USR-8F4K29 — never your phone number.

2

Verify your phone number

We send a short one-time code that expires quickly and is checked on our servers. Verification is required before you can transact.

3

Choose your amount

Anywhere from the minimum to the maximum shown on the calculator. The illustrative figures in force are recorded against your investment.

4

Make the deposit

Send the exact amount to the receiving account shown on your deposit page. The account details are set by our administrators, not hard-coded.

5

Submit your proof

Enter the transaction ID and attach the confirmation screenshot. Your submission is recorded as a claim, not as money received.

6

We verify the payment

Our team matches the reference, the amount and the sending account against our actual receiving records. A screenshot alone is never treated as proof.

7

Your balance is credited

Once verified, a permanent ledger entry credits your available balance, with a reference you can trace on your transactions page.

8

Monitor your account

Balances, deposits, withdrawals, every ledger movement and every sign-in are visible to you in your dashboard.

9

Request a withdrawal

Request from your available balance in the app, or send the request over WhatsApp. The amount is held while our finance team reviews it.
